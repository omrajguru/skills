# Image references — sources and credits

Collected 24 October 2026. The images themselves are not stored in this repository. `scripts/refs.py fetch` downloads each one from the direct image URL listed below, on the user's machine, and builds contact sheets of them in `_boards/`. The contact sheets are not additional references.

Creator credits distinguish the original creator from the website that supplied the image. Unverified individual credits are explicitly marked rather than guessed. A source-page title is not necessarily the title of the underlying video. Some references are thumbnails, published styleframes, mood photographs, or static frames from GIFs. No video timestamps have been invented.

## Rights and provenance

Credits do not grant reuse rights. This is a visual reference collection, not a licensed production asset library. Rights in films, music videos, gameplay, interfaces, and photographs remain with their respective creators. Consult each source for its terms before public reuse. The two Rawpixel references marked AI-generated are identified as such by their source.
